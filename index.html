How ratings work

  • GreenHigh confidence unless tagged otherwise. Two or more independent sources agree: about 4.3+ on a meaningful sample, or positive press plus 4.2+, and no recurring serious complaint.
  • YellowMixed or middling (about 3.5–4.2), big disagreement between platforms, recurring service or consistency complaints, or thin/dated evidence.
  • RedMostly negative recent evidence (about 3.4 or below), repeated sickness reports, or a cited failed inspection.
  • GreenLow confidence, 1 source Only one free source covers this location. Same thresholds, but green or red needs 20+ reviews on that source; otherwise yellow. Shown with an outlined pill and a hollow map dot. Use the Confidence filter to hide these.
  • UnknownNo usable source for this location (no listing, no ratings yet, or the listing may be a different business). Never guessed.
  • ClosedA citable source says it closed. Not rated, kept on the map.
  • Not yet ratedIn the OSM dataset, but no research run has covered it yet.

    Sources & honesty notes

    • Restaurant list: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), via the Overpass API. Snapshot: .
    • ZIP backfill and ZIP centers: US Census 2020 ZCTA Gazetteer. About a third of ZIPs are inferred from the nearest ZIP center, so some may be wrong.
    • Cuisine comes from OSM tags. Where those are missing it is inferred from the name, or listed as Other/Unknown.
    • Star figures in reasons are as aggregators (Restaurantji, Birdeye, Yahoo Local and others) reported them, usually second-hand from Google or Yelp, so treat them as approximate.
    • Reddit, Yelp and Tripadvisor pages were blocked from our research machine, and X search reaches only about 30 days back, so they were used only through search results. Free sources only: no paid or keyed review APIs.
    • Basemap: OpenStreetMap HOT style by the Humanitarian OSM Team, hosted free by OpenStreetMap France, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
